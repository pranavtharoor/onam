# Self-critique: skeptical creative director pass

Run before calling any scene, concept or storyboard done. Answer in writing;
"probably fine" is a fail.

## Is it generic?
- Could this frame belong to any other festival, brand or country if the flowers were swapped? What makes it *Kerala*, specifically?
- If I describe it in one sentence, does that sentence contain a noun only Onam has (thumba, nadumuttam, the leaf's left-pointing tip, ada pradhaman)?
- Does it trip any `frontend-design` tell (cream + serif + terracotta; eyebrow labels; middle-dot meta strings; one italic word in a headline)?

## Does it look AI-generated?
- Symmetry that is too perfect, gradients that are too smooth, lighting with no source, textures that repeat visibly.
- Objects that are "flower-like" rather than a named flower; "vessel-like" rather than an uruli.
- Uniform stroke weights and perfect curves where a hand would waver.

## Is it culturally superficial?
- Are motifs used for what they mean (pookalam growth, Sadhya order, Maveli's return) or only for how they look?
- Anything that a Malayali grandmother would correct? (leaf direction, rangoli-for-pookalam, diya-for-nilavilakku, wrong script.)
- Is Malayalam used as meaning, verified — or as texture?

## Is it over-decorated?
- Remove one element (Chanel's rule). Is the frame better? Then remove it.
- Is there more than one "hero" competing in the frame?

## Is it technically impressive but emotionally empty?
- What does the visitor *feel* at this moment, and what makes them feel it? If the answer is "wow, a pinned section", cut or rethink.
- Where is a person, a hand, a trace of a household? Invitations are from people.

## Is it repetitive?
- Is this the third reveal done the same way? List every transition in order; no two adjacent ones may use the same device.
- Do all headlines enter the same way?

## Is it derivative of award-site patterns?
- Giant split-text headline flying in letter by letter; horizontal-scroll gallery of cards; cursor-following blob; WebGL distortion on hover; marquee ticker; preloader counting to 100%. Any of these needs a reason that is about Onam, or it goes.

Record what you cut and what replaced it in the brief's decision log.
